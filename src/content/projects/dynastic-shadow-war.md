Dynastic Shadow War is built around the tension between what a player can see and what they can infer. Before battle, players know the color composition of an opposing army but not its unit identities, loyalties, or King. That narrow window of information creates room for secret reformation, bluffing, and deduction before every simultaneous reveal.
