Recently I have done 90% of the combat system. Now it looks like a ready-to-go system as a full. I do have confidence that player will enjoy different creatures with different trait, and the combination of them, because I do as a player myself.

But the very one thing just can't let go of my head: is the world interesting enough?

My idea was to create an RPG game, where human and grid beasts live together as a whole. It was a 2D world that player can explore around and catch beasts in it. Then I realised it was so poorly built so I decided to switch to the 3D world I currently work on. 

I tried to make it look better but it just kind of not.

And now I start to question myself, do we actually need a world that is significantly worse than one of its systems?

I don't know now but will find out soon.
